## 👋 About Me

I'm **Zeynep Can**, a Management Information Systems graduate focused on
**Business Analysis, ERP & Business Systems**.

I enjoy understanding how business processes work, identifying needs and
turning them into structured requirements, workflows and testable solutions.

During my internship experiences, I worked with data and reporting tools such
as **SQL, Power BI and Excel**, while gaining exposure to ERP-based processes
in **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations** and **caniasERP** environments.

Today, I continue building practical Business Analysis case studies and sharing
what I learn through my portfolio, GitHub projects and Medium articles.
